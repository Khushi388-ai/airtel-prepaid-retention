# 07. Business Rules

Thresholds are illustrative and need validation with stakeholders.

| ID | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| BRU1 | No plan may be withdrawn or repriced without a churn-risk estimate | Prevents blind decisions (BR1) |
| BRU2 | Affected customers must be notified at least 7 days before withdrawal | Removes the surprise (BR2) |
| BRU3 | Every withdrawal must offer at least one alternative at or near the old price | Gives price-sensitive users a choice (BR4) |
| BRU4 | Churn for affected segments is reviewed weekly for 8 weeks after a change | Faster detection than quarterly results (BR3) |
| BRU5 | If weekly churn rises more than 0.2 percentage points above baseline, a review is mandatory | Sets the trigger in advance (BR5) |
| BRU6 | Every reversal or adjustment is logged with its reason and the data used | Builds a learning record (BR5) |
